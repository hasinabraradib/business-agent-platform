# Nodi Kitchen (নদী কিচেন)

Nodi Kitchen serves home-style Bengali food in Dhanmondi, Dhaka. Our kitchen cooks with mustard
oil, fresh river fish and spices ground in-house every morning.

## Opening hours

We are open every day from 12:00 noon to 11:00 pm. On Fridays we open at 2:30 pm, after Jumu'ah
prayers. The kitchen takes its last orders at 10:30 pm.

During Ramadan we open at 4:00 pm and serve an iftar platter from sunset; we close at 11:30 pm.
We are closed on the first day of Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha.

## Location and parking

House 27, Road 9/A, Dhanmondi, Dhaka 1209. We are on the first floor, above a pharmacy; there is
a lift. Limited parking for six cars is available in front of the building after 6 pm. The
nearest landmark is the Dhanmondi Lake footbridge, about five minutes on foot.

## Reservations

You can reserve a table by calling 01700-000000 between 11 am and 10 pm. We hold a reservation
for 15 minutes after the booked time. Groups of eight or more must pay a deposit of 1,000 BDT,
which is deducted from the bill; the deposit is refunded if you cancel at least 24 hours ahead.
We do not take reservations for Friday lunch; seating is first come, first served.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the food halal?

Yes. All meat and poultry is halal, and we do not serve alcohol or pork.

### Do you deliver?

We deliver within 5 km of the restaurant through our own riders, from 1 pm to 10 pm. Orders over
1,500 BDT get free delivery; otherwise delivery costs 60 BDT.

### How can I pay?

We accept cash, Visa and Mastercard, bKash and Nagad. A 5% VAT is added to every bill. There is
no service charge, and tips are optional.

### Can you adjust the spice level?

Most curries can be made milder on request. Shorshe Ilish and Beef Kala Bhuna are traditionally
hot and cannot be made fully mild. Tell your server about allergies; our kitchen handles nuts,
dairy, shellfish and mustard, so we cannot guarantee that any dish is free of traces.

### Do you have food for children and vegetarians?

Yes. We have a children's half portion of Morog Polao for 220 BDT, and our vegetable dishes and
dal are vegan. High chairs are available.

### খাবার কি বাসায় নেওয়া যাবে?

হ্যাঁ, সব খাবার পার্সেল করে নেওয়া যায়। পার্সেলের জন্য অতিরিক্ত কোনো চার্জ নেই। বড় অর্ডারের জন্য
অন্তত দুই ঘণ্টা আগে ফোন করুন।

### জন্মদিন বা অনুষ্ঠানের জন্য কি জায়গা পাওয়া যায়?

আমাদের একটি আলাদা কক্ষে সর্বোচ্চ ২০ জন বসতে পারেন। অন্তত তিন দিন আগে বুকিং দিতে হবে।
