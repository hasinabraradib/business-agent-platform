# Frequently asked questions

## Are your products really handmade?

Yes. Our jamdani sarees are woven on handlooms in Rupganj, our kantha is stitched by women's
cooperatives in Jessore, and our jute goods are made in small workshops in Faridpur. Small
irregularities in weave or stitching are a sign of handwork, not a defect.

## How do I care for a jamdani saree?

Dry-clean the first few times. After that, a gentle hand wash in cold water with mild soap is
fine; never wring it. Dry it in the shade and store it folded in cotton cloth, refolding every
few months so the creases do not weaken the threads.
