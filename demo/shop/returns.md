# Returns and refunds

We want you to love what you buy. If something is not right, you can return it within 7 days of
delivery.

## What can be returned

Items must be unused, unwashed and in their original packaging with tags attached. Sarees must
not have a fall or pico attached. For hygiene reasons, gamchas cannot be returned once opened.

Made-to-order items, sale items marked "final sale" and gift boxes cannot be returned unless
they arrive damaged or wrong.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your parcel arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, message us within 48 hours with
photos of the item and the packaging. We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or a
full refund, including the delivery charge.

## How refunds work

Once we receive and check the returned item, we refund within 5 working days. Payments made by
bKash or Nagad are refunded to the same number; card payments go back to the card and may take
up to 10 days to appear. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bKash. The original delivery
charge is not refunded for change-of-mind returns.

## Exchanges

You can exchange an item for a different colour or size within 7 days, subject to stock. We
cover the return pickup for the first exchange.
