# Shipping

Jamdani Lane ships from our studio in Mirpur, Dhaka. Orders placed before 2 pm (Saturday to
Thursday) are packed the same day.

## Delivery times and charges

| Area | Time | Charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inside Dhaka city | 1–2 working days | 70 BDT |
| Dhaka suburbs (Savar, Gazipur, Narayanganj) | 2–3 working days | 100 BDT |
| Rest of Bangladesh | 3–5 working days | 130 BDT |

Orders over 5,000 BDT ship free anywhere in Bangladesh. Made-to-order items ship in 10–14 days.

## Couriers and tracking

We use Pathao and Steadfast inside Bangladesh. You receive an SMS with a tracking link when your
parcel leaves the studio.

## Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 15,000 BDT. For larger orders, or for sarees above
10,000 BDT, we ask for an advance payment of 50% by bKash or card.

## International shipping

We ship to India, the UK, the USA, Canada and the Middle East by DHL Express, usually in 5–8
working days. International charges are calculated at checkout by weight. Customs duties and
taxes in the destination country are paid by the customer.
