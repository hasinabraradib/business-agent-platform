# Saffron & Sage Bistro: Frequently asked questions

Saffron & Sage Bistro is a neighbourhood restaurant in Austin, Texas, serving seasonal
Mediterranean-meets-Texas cooking.

## Opening hours

- Monday: closed.
- Tuesday to Thursday: 11:30 am to 9:30 pm.
- Friday and Saturday: 11:30 am to 10:30 pm (weekend brunch Saturday 10:00 am to 2:30 pm).
- Sunday: 10:00 am to 9:00 pm (brunch until 2:30 pm).

The kitchen takes last orders 30 minutes before closing. We are closed on Thanksgiving Day and
Christmas Day.

## Where are you?

2214 South Lamar Boulevard, Austin, TX 78704, between Oltorf Street and Bluebonnet Lane. We are
in the white brick building with the green awning.

## Is there parking?

Yes. We have a free 18-space lot behind the building (enter from Bluebonnet Lane). Street parking
on South Lamar is free after 6 pm. There are two accessible spaces next to the rear entrance and
bike racks by the front door.

## Do you have vegan and gluten-free options?

Yes. Every dish on the menu is labelled vegan (V), vegetarian (VG) or gluten-free (GF). Our most
popular vegan dish is the Roasted Cauliflower Shawarma. Many dishes can be adapted; ask your
server.

## What is your allergy policy?

Tell your server about any allergy when you order and we will tell you what is safe. Our kitchen
handles tree nuts, peanuts, sesame, dairy, eggs, wheat, shellfish and fish, so we cannot
guarantee that any dish is completely free of traces. Fries are cooked in a dedicated fryer.
For severe allergies, please call us before your visit at (512) 555-0142.

## Do you host private events?

Yes. Our private dining room, The Sage Room, seats up to 20 guests for birthdays, rehearsal
dinners and business dinners, with a set menu from $55 per person. Book it at least one week in
advance by calling (512) 555-0142 or emailing events@saffronandsage.example.

## Can I bring my own cake?

Yes, for birthdays and celebrations. There is a $3 per guest cake-cutting fee.

## Are children welcome?

Yes. We have high chairs and a kids' plate (grilled chicken or pasta with fries) for $9.

## Do you offer takeout or delivery?

Takeout, yes: order by phone at (512) 555-0142 and pick up at the host stand. We do not deliver
ourselves and are not on delivery apps.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are available at the host stand in any amount from $25.

## How do I reach you?

Call (512) 555-0142 (11 am to 9 pm, Tuesday to Sunday) or email hello@saffronandsage.example.
