# Saffron & Sage Bistro: Menu

Seasonal Mediterranean-meets-Texas cooking from our open kitchen on South Lamar. Prices are in US
dollars and include no service charge. Labels: **(V)** vegan, **(VG)** vegetarian, **(GF)**
gluten-free. Ask your server about allergens; see our allergy policy in the FAQ.

## Small plates

- **Whipped Feta & Hot Honey** (VG) - $12. Whipped feta, Texas wildflower honey with chile, pistachio dukkah, warm flatbread.
- **Charred Carrot Hummus** (V) - $11. Smoky carrot hummus, za'atar, toasted pepitas, cucumber, house pita. Ask for GF crudités instead of pita.
- **Crispy Brussels Sprouts** (V, GF) - $10. Pomegranate molasses, sumac, mint.
- **Gulf Shrimp a la Plancha** (GF) - $16. Garlic, Aleppo pepper, lemon, saffron aioli.
- **Lamb Kofta Skewers** (GF) - $15. Two skewers, tahini yogurt, pickled red onion.
