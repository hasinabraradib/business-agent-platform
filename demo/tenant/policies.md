# Saffron & Sage Bistro: Reservation and dining policies

## Reservations

- You can book a table online, through the chat on our website, for parties of up to 8 guests.
- For parties of 9 or more, please call us at (512) 555-0142; larger groups may need a set menu.
- We hold reservations for 15 minutes after the booked time. After that, the table may be given
  to waiting guests.
- Walk-ins are always welcome; bar seating is first come, first served.

## Cancellations and changes

- Please cancel or change your reservation at least 24 hours in advance.
- Cancellations within 24 hours, and no-shows, are charged $15 per guest for parties of 6 or
  more.
- To cancel or change a reservation, call (512) 555-0142 or reply to your confirmation message.

## Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept checks.
Groups of 8 or more have an automatic 20% gratuity. Austin sales tax is added to every bill.

## Dress code

None. Come as you are.

## Pets

Leashed dogs are welcome on the patio. Only service animals are allowed inside.
